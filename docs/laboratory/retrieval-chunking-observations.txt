Retrieval Chunking Laboratory — Reading Observation
===================================================

Scope
-----
This is an experiment-local SQLite fixture. It does not modify the production
database, migrations, API, semantic index, or Conversation storage.

Input and rule
--------------
- 3 Turns
- T2 is a 1,741 UTF-16-character Japanese technical explanation.
- Turn boundaries are hard boundaries.
- Blank-line paragraphs are DB-specific hard boundaries.
- 。！？ are candidate sentence boundaries.
- Quoted punctuation is tracked so 「これは保存されます。」と言った remains
  in one sentence candidate.
- Sentence candidates are packed with minimum 120, target 220, maximum 360.
- Original Turn text remains authoritative; segments retain Turn ID and offsets.

Observed result
---------------
- 47 sentence candidates became 11 retrieval segments.
- Sentence-only extraction is often too short to identify meaning:
  - 「これは保存されます。」: 10 characters; the conserved quantity is unknown.
  - 「重要です。」: 5 characters; the important subject is unknown.
  - 「この比を十分小さくします。」: 13 characters; the ratio is unknown.
- Paragraph-based segments of 152–218 characters were readable in this fixture.
  Momentum, energy, measurement, and approximation remained separate, and each
  demonstrative had a nearby definition.
- T2-S2 identified 「これは保存されます。」 as total momentum P.
- T2-S3 identified 「これも保存されます。」 as energy quantity Q.
- T2-S4 retained the definition of the camera interval/period ratio around
  「重要です。」.
- T2-S7 retained epsilon=abs(x)/ell around 「この比を十分小さくします。」.

Meaning-loss case
-----------------
The long measurement-error paragraph was split by length into T2-S5 and T2-S6.
T2-S5 defines residual R. T2-S6 begins with the next step and later says
「その値」 and R, but it no longer contains the definition of R. The latter
segment is broadly understandable as an error-analysis procedure, but it is not
self-contained enough for a precise retrieval result.

Assessment
----------
1. A raw 。 unit is useful as a boundary candidate, not as the final retrieval
   payload.
2. Joining short sentences within a topic paragraph materially improves meaning.
3. Length control alone can still cut between a definition and its later use.
4. A practical next rule should preserve a small overlap, repeat the paragraph
   heading as metadata, or expand the selected hit by one previous sentence at
   retrieval time.
5. Short complete Turns such as T1 and T3 should remain below the minimum rather
   than cross a Turn boundary.
